### Squeeze (radial compression of o-ring):
\[\%Squeeze=\frac{W_{o-ring}-W_{c}}{W_{o-ring}}*100\]

Guidelines:<br>
Static 10-25%<br>
Dynamic 8-16%<br>
Face 20-30%<br>

### Stretch (tangential stretch of o-ring in groove):
\[\%stretch=\frac{D_{groove}-D_{inner\:ring}}{D_{inner\:ring}}*100\]

Guideline: 1-5%


### Gland fill:
\[\%fill=\frac{A_{ring}+(n_{BUR}*{A_{BUR}})}{A_{gland}}*100\]

Guideline: 60-85%
